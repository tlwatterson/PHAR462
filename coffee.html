The Conference Coffee Dilemma

You are attending a professional conference. Your first session starts in 15 minutes, and you realize you forgot to get coffee.

What do you do?

The coffee line is very long. Another attendee says: 'Looks like everyone had the same idea.'

You skip coffee and arrive early. Front-row seats are available, and colleagues are sitting near the back.

Ending A

You gain coffee and make a new professional connection.

Ending B

You successfully obtain coffee and arrive as the session begins.

Ending C

You meet the speaker before the session starts.

Ending D

You learn about interesting projects from colleagues.

Debrief

  1. Was there a single correct answer?
  2. What information was revealed by different choices?
  3. How is this similar to patient encounters?

Key takeaway: Different questions uncover different information.
